About Gabardine Echo

An independent fashion guide for people who value cloth, cut and clothes that stay with them for years.

Our story

A trench coat with a past

Gabardine Echo began with a second-hand trench coat, decades old and still perfectly crisp. Its fabric had outlasted countless fast-fashion pieces, and that made us curious: what was this cloth, and why did the design still feel modern?

Finding answers led us to the history of weaving, the craft of tailoring and the way classic styles return generation after generation. This website shares what we learned.

Independent

We do not sell clothing and are not affiliated with any brand, designer or retailer.

Cloth and cut

We focus on fabric and fit, the two things that make clothes look good for years.

Wear it longer

We encourage fewer, better purchases, careful maintenance and tailoring alterations.

How we work

Research and craft knowledge

Our guides draw on textile and fashion-history references, fibre and care-labelling information and conversations with tailors and dressmakers. We describe how fabrics and garments generally behave rather than reviewing or ranking products.
